# Data shipped with qlpca

`st_gender_eu15.csv` - the data set of chapter 4 of the PhD thesis (Lavado, 2012,
Appendix A.1): 11 indicators of gender differences in science and technology for
15 EU countries, 2003-2004. Origin: compiled by Oliveira & Carvalho (2009) from Eurostat
(and *She Figures 2006*), as used in chapter 4 of the thesis. Load it with
`qlpca.datasets.load_st_gender()`.

These data are **not** covered by the MIT licence of the code. Origin, variables and
conditions of use: `data/README.md` in the repository,
https://github.com/nlavadodatasci/quasi-linear-PCA/blob/main/data/README.md
